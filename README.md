# 🍅 Tutomato

**Lock in to ripen up: a Pomodoro study timer for students.**

## Problem Statement

Many students sit down to study with good intentions, then lose an hour to their phones, a messy to-do list, or plain burnout. The Pomodoro technique helps by splitting work into focused sessions with short breaks, but most timer apps only count down. They don't make studying feel rewarding, and they don't show how far you've come. Without that bit of motivation, it's easy to quit after a few days.

Tutomato turns focus time into something students actually want to keep doing, with a timer that fits their style, points they can earn, and friends to keep them going.

## Objectives

Tutomato is a friendly, motivating Pomodoro app that helps students stay focused and consistent in their studies. Specifically, it aims to:

1. Provide a flexible timer with study and break options that users can pick based on how they like to work.
2. Reward focus sessions with Study Points that users can spend on customizing their character.
3. Help users track their progress and plan what to study next through a progress tracker and a "To Learn" list.
4. Help users build study habits together through shared streaks with the Pomodori character.

## Target Users

Students, particularly those who struggle with procrastination, distractions, and managing their study time.

## Features and Scope

- **Pomodoro timer:** Run focus sessions and short breaks using the standard 25/5 cycle, or set custom durations (`TODO: specify the allowed range`).
- **Lock-in mode:** During a focus session, the app encourages users to stay on the app. A session counts as complete only if the user does not leave the app or cancel the timer before it ends.
- **Study Points:** Earned by completing focus sessions and spent on character customization.
- **Progress tracker and "To Learn" list:** Review past sessions and plan what to study next.
- **Shared streaks:** Build habits together with the Pomodori character.

## Limitations

- **No guaranteed focus.** The app can detect whether the user stays in the app, but it cannot verify that they are actually studying. A student may leave the timer running while doing something else.
- **No app or website blocking.** The app will not block other applications, due to operating system restrictions and limited development time.
- **Single platform.** The app is developed and tested on Android only. There is no iOS or web version.
